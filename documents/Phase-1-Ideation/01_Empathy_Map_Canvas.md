### 1. Objective of this Canvas
The purpose of this Empathy Map Canvas is to deeply understand the actual everyday behaviors, emotional triggers, frustrations, and practical needs of our primary users:
1. **The Guest / Traveller** (Persona: Aarav, 24, young professional & Meera, 38, family trip planner).
2. **The Small / Independent Hotelier** (Persona: Rakesh, 45, manages a 20-room property in North Goa).

By examining what they see, say, do, hear, think, and feel, we uncover the root causes of booking failures (such as double bookings and unexpected checkout charges) and design features that solve real human pain points.

### 4. Key Takeaways for HotelPro Implementation
1. **The 10-Minute Hold Window is Non-Negotiable**: Guests need peace of mind while opening payment apps; hoteliers need automated expiry so rooms don't sit blocked indefinitely.
2. **Transparent Server-Side Price Calculation**: Eliminate checkout surprise by displaying the full base price, duration, and statutory 12% GST clearly at every step.
3. **AI Grounded in Live Catalog**: The AI assistant must not guess or hallucinate; it must query verified amenities and real-time availability directly from the HotelPro system.
